FINAL DOMAIN
-------------
Current placeholder domain:
https://are-sty-x.vercel.app

Before deploying to another domain, replace this domain in:
- canonical tags in HTML
- hreflang tags in HTML
- sitemap.xml
- robots.txt

SEARCH CONSOLE
--------------
Do not invent a verification token.
Use the exact verification method/token generated by Google Search Console.

ADSENSE
-------
Do not invent a publisher ID.
After AdSense gives you your real publisher ID, update ads.txt using Google's exact line.
Then add the AdSense code from your AdSense account to the site.

CONTACT
-------
Replace contact@example.com in contact.html with your real email address.

INDEXING
--------
No website package can guarantee Google indexing or ranking.
Submit sitemap.xml in Google Search Console after deployment and request indexing for important URLs.
